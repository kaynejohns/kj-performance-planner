The real problem

You don't need a harder plan.
You need an honest one.

Three things that derail most athletes — every single year — and almost nobody names them out loud.

01
Chasing a time you're not ready for
The race date is 12 weeks away and the goal time assumes a fitness level you haven't built yet. Effort can't compress physiology. Most "failed" race blocks aren't failures — they were unrealistic from week one.
02
Following plans that ignore your history
Generic plans assume an average athlete with no injury history, full availability, and perfect consistency. You have a calf issue, six hours a week, and life that doesn't always cooperate. The plan breaks. So do you.
03
Ramping volume too fast and breaking down
More kilometres, more sessions, more intensity — without the load tolerance to absorb it. Then injury. Then time off. Then starting from zero again. It's not bad luck. It's the wrong progression.
